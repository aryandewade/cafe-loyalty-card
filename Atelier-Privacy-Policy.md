## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run your loyalty membership.

**2.1 Account Data**
- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Password (stored in encrypted/hashed form; we cannot see your actual password)

**2.2 Membership & Loyalty Data**
- Your member Card ID
- Stamps earned, points balance, and member tier
- Rewards claimed, redemption codes, and redemption status

**2.3 Visit & Purchase Data**
- Items ordered, amount spent, date and time of visit
- Café outlet visited and the barista who served you

**2.4 Preference Data (Optional)**
- Beverage, roast, and milk preferences, so we can personalise your visits

**2.5 Technical Data**
- Device type, browser, IP address, and usage information
- Data stored in your browser (such as cookies and local storage) so the Platform works properly (see Section 8)

- We do not collect your card, UPI, or bank details through the Platform. [Edit this line if online payments are added; payments at the café counter are handled by our payment partner at the till.]

## 3. Why We Use Your Data

We use your data only for the purposes below, and only with your consent or where the law permits:

1. Creating and managing your membership account
2. Recording stamps, points, and visits, and issuing rewards
3. Generating your Pass QR and reward redemption codes, and verifying them at the counter
4. Showing your visit history and receipts
5. Personalising your experience based on your stated preferences
6. Sending service messages (account, security, reward and redemption alerts)
7. Sending offers and updates, **only if you opt in** (see Section 5)
8. Responding to your questions and complaints
9. Preventing fraud, misuse, and duplicate or false redemptions
10. Meeting legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We will not use your data for a new purpose without telling you and, where required, taking your fresh consent.

## 4. Your Consent

- We ask for your consent in clear, simple language before collecting your data, through an unticked checkbox at registration.
- Consent for marketing is **separate** from consent for running your account. You can use the loyalty club without agreeing to marketing messages.
- You can **withdraw your consent at any time** by writing to our Grievance Officer or by using the unsubscribe or account settings option. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide membership features. Anything we did lawfully before you withdrew remains valid.

## 5. Marketing Messages

- We send promotional emails, SMS, or notifications only if you have opted in.
- Every promotional message includes a simple way to opt out.
- Service messages (such as reward redemption confirmations or security alerts) are not marketing and may still be sent while your account is active.

## 10. How We Protect Your Data

We use reasonable security safeguards, as required under Section 43A of the IT Act and the SPDI Rules, including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Hashed passwords
- Role-based access, so staff see only what they need
- Time-limited reward redemption codes
- Regular review of our security practices

No online system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Please keep your password and your Pass QR private, and tell us immediately if you suspect misuse of your account.

## 12. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

1. **Access:** know what personal data of yours we hold and how it is used.
2. **Correction:** ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (for example, your phone number or preferences, which you can also edit in your Profile).
3. **Erasure:** ask us to delete your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
4. **Withdraw consent:** at any time, as explained in Section 4.
5. **Grievance redressal:** complain to our Grievance Officer and receive a response.
6. **Nominate:** name another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.

To use any of these rights, write to [Grievance Officer Email]. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the **Data Protection Board of India**.
